Why FinPay

Money software you can actually trust.

Designed from a rigorous detailed design record — every decision reviewed, every edge case accounted for.

🧮

Paise-exact maths

No floating point, ever. Balances and cashback are 64-bit integers in paise, with floor truncation — 6.66 paise becomes 6.

🔁

Duplicate-proof payments

Every payment carries a unique idempotency key. Tap twice or retry on a bad network — you're only ever charged once.

🎯

Monthly cashback caps

Track your progress per category against transparent monthly caps, with a live preview before you pay.

🔐

HTTP-only sessions

Signed JWTs live in HttpOnly, SameSite cookies — out of reach of injected scripts — with server-side role checks.

🕵️

Masked by design

Coordinators see what they need and nothing more: contact details are masked in every admin response.

📜

Append-only audit trail

Payments, top-ups, tier changes and rule edits are written to an immutable log for dispute resolution.
